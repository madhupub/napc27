The Conference
About NAPC 2027

NAPC 2027 marks the sixth edition of the National Aerospace Propulsion Conference, a flagship biennial event themed 'Advancements in Propulsion Systems for Next-Gen Aerospace Vehicles'. This prestigious gathering brings together scientists, engineers, and researchers from defence laboratories, space agencies, academia, and industry to explore cutting-edge innovations in sustainable, high-performance propulsion.

The conference covers a broad spectrum of propulsion technologies — from gas turbines and ramjets for aircraft and missiles, to liquid, solid, and hybrid rockets for space launch vehicles, to emerging concepts such as electric propulsion, distributed propulsion for eVTOLs, and AI-driven combustion diagnostics. NAPC 2027 is hosted by the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, IIT Hyderabad, under the auspices of the National Council for Air Breathing Engines (NCABE), in collaboration with DRDO and ISRO.

About IIT Hyderabad

The Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (IITH) is among the second generation of IITs established by the Government of India. Located at Kandi, Sangareddy district, Telangana, IITH has rapidly grown into a leading research and teaching institution. It is recognised for its forward-looking academic programs, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and a culture of interdisciplinary innovation.

IITH maintains strong collaborative ties with DRDO, ISRO, HAL, NAL and premier international universities. The campus hosts a world-class Convention Centre equipped for large international conferences, making it an ideal venue for NAPC 2027.

Section A
Call for Papers

NAPC 2027 invites original research contributions from academia, industry, defence laboratories, and space agencies across all areas of aerospace propulsion. Authors are invited to submit papers on topics covering gas turbine propulsion, rocket propulsion, high-speed propulsion, electric and hybrid propulsion, and related special topics. All submissions must present original, unpublished work.

Section B
Abstract Submission, Full Paper Guidelines & Review Process
Abstract Submission

Authors must first submit a structured abstract for review. The abstract must clearly state the motivation, methodology, key results, and significance of the work. All abstracts are reviewed for relevance and originality before full paper invitation.

  • Word limit: 300–500 words
  • Must include: Title, Author name(s), Affiliation(s), Keywords
  • Clearly indicate the technical track / vertical
  • Submit via the Microsoft CMT portal (see Section C below)
  • Deadline: 15 August 2026
Full Paper Guidelines

Authors whose abstracts are accepted will be invited to submit the full-length paper. The paper must follow the official NAPC 2027 template and adhere strictly to the formatting and length requirements listed below.

  • Page limit: 4–6 pages in the NAPC format (including figures, tables, and references)
  • Use the official NAPC 2027 manuscript template (available in Downloads)
  • All figures must be high resolution (minimum 300 DPI), embedded in the document
  • Use SI units throughout; define all abbreviations at first use
  • References must follow the NAPC style guide included in the template
  • Include structured sections: Introduction, Methodology, Results, Conclusions
  • Deadline: 30 September 2026

Note to Authors: The downloadable full paper template will be uploaded here immediately after abstract submissions close. Please check back then to format your final manuscript.

General Guidelines: Only original, unpublished work will be accepted. Plagiarism in any form (including self-plagiarism) will lead to immediate rejection. Authors are responsible for ensuring the scientific accuracy and ethical integrity of their submissions. Papers that have been published or are under review elsewhere will not be considered. All authors must have made a genuine contribution to the work.

Review Process

All submissions undergo a rigorous double-blind peer review process by domain experts from DRDO, ISRO, IITs, and the industry. The review committee evaluates submissions for technical merit, novelty, clarity, and relevance to the conference scope.

  • Review type: Double-blind peer review
  • Reviewers are domain experts from DRDO, ISRO, IITs & Industry
  • Abstract acceptance notification: 15 August 2026
  • Full paper acceptance notification: 15 November 2026
